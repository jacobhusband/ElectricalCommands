## 3. General & Schedule Notes

1. **Color Temperature (CCT)**: All general lighting luminaires shall be **3500K CCT** and **85+ CRI (or JA8 Title 24 compliant)** unless specifically directed otherwise by the Architect.
2. **Room Controllers**: Contractor shall provide the appropriate number of room controllers (`LMRC-211`, `LMRC-212`, `LMPL-201`) for the project. Coordinate with the manufacturer for the correct quantity and placement.
3. **Junction Boxes**: Contractor shall provide 4"x4" deep junction boxes as required for above-ceiling controllers and connections.
4. **Sensor Placement**: Ceiling sensors shall be located for optimum space coverage and mounted at least 48" away from HVAC supply air diffusers per Title 24 standards.
5. **Low-Voltage Cabling**: Provide pre-terminated Cat5e/Cat6 DLM cables between room controllers, digital switches, and sensors.

## 4. Drawing Analysis & Submittal Review Rules

### A. Single-Face vs. Double-Face Exit / Directional Fixtures
- **Plan & RCP Symbol Distinction**:
  - **Single-Face Fixture**: The symbol on the architectural RCP (`A04-xx`) or electrical lighting plan (`E02.00`) displays a solid hatch in **one quadrant**.
  - **Double-Face Fixture**: The symbol on the architectural RCP (`A04-xx`) or electrical lighting plan (`E02.00`) displays a solid hatch in **two opposite quadrants**.
- **Submittal Verification**: Ensure the submitted fixture catalog model matches the single-face (`EDGR-1-R-EL`) or double-face (`EDGR-2-R-EL`) count shown on the plans.

### B. Emergency Lighting Architecture (Integral Battery Packs vs. Central Inverter)
- **Integral Emergency Battery Packs**:
  - Fixtures designated with `E`, `-EM`, or internal 90-minute battery pack (e.g. `R-14E`, `R-19E`, `HE`).
  - Require unswitched normal sense/charging line connected ahead of local switches.
- **Central Battery Inverter / Micro Inverter Systems**:
  - Inverter system powers designated emergency fixtures during outages (e.g. `R-16E` note: *"EMERGENCY FIXTURE CONTROLLED VIA MICRO INVERTER"*).
  - **Review Procedure**: Cross-examine the **Lighting Plan (`E02.00`)**, **Lighting Fixture Schedule (`E05.00`)**, and the **Power Plan (`E01.00`) / Single Line Diagram (`E07.00`)** to locate the central inverter unit, verify its electrical capacity/feed, and confirm dedicated emergency circuit distribution.
